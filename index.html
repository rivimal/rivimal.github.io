About

From communication networks and FPGA R&D to autonomous multi-robot systems.

I am a Doctoral Researcher at the Turku Intelligent Embedded and Robotic Systems (TIERS) Lab, University of Turku. My current work investigates communication architectures and communication-aware control and navigation for multi-robot systems used in remote monitoring applications.

I hold an M.Sc. (Tech.) in Information and Communication Technology, majoring in Robotics and Autonomous Systems with a minor in Machine Learning, from the University of Turku. Before moving into robotics research, I worked in industrial R&D at Synopsys and in mobile network operations at Dialog Axiata.
